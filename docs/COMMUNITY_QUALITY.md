# Community discovery quality and usability

Scope and acceptance agreed by the reported defects (2026-11-06):
- Focus: replace the square inner outline on the header search and language/theme controls with one rounded container indicator. Retain visible keyboard focus on all controls and forced-colors support.
- Waiting: keep the source request budget. Expose bounded remaining seconds from the server, disable live recollection until due, preserve results, and show a neutral inline countdown instead of a global red error. Archive browsing remains available.
- Live quality: require at least two distinct known endorsers, more positive than critical sampled comments, plus current public YouTube metadata meeting editable minimum views/likes (defaults 100,000/1,000) and selected declared audio language. Verify through the user's metered browser client, never send the key to the local classifier. Missing key/metadata, unavailable statistics and analysis failure cannot produce an apparently verified result. Preserve community score ordering after filtering. No subscriber-count quality claim.
- Popularity and evidence are distinct: display fetched views/likes, separate endorser count and contextual community score. A score of one is not a popularity measure. No inference that model classification or high popularity guarantees quality/authenticity.
- Clear entry to original HackerTube library with original ordering, source quotes and same-recommender links. A short current in-page guide replaces the obsolete manual-batch instructions. Do not restore manual batch clicking or present generic archive imports as live source searches.
- Deterministic RED/GREEN tests, defect plants, full project check, independent security review and browser checks with screenshots. Existing catalog/brand assets remain unchanged.

Audit evidence: user-provided screenshots show square inner focus rings, red generic cooldown, and a 49-view/0-like video with one mention. Current browser screenshots 01-community-before and 02-focus-before reproduce hidden archive entry and the focus mismatch. Code confirms automatic community sessions filtered only score>0, with no YouTube popularity gate; cooldown was a source-wide 60-second floor without a remaining-duration response. The supplied explanation describes earlier manual controls removed by the automatic workflow. These are measured behavior gaps, not user error.

## Delivered contracts

MEASURED: `server/community-collector.js` keeps its one-minute per-source floor and bounded provider backoff. Successful results carry retryAfterSeconds; a cooldown refusal returns only the typed code and validated integer seconds. No error message, token or provider payload is exposed. The browser countdown is advisory; the server remains authoritative after reload or clock changes.

MEASURED: `rankEvidence` retains historical recommendation-month scoring and raw recommender counts, and adds independentRecommenders (known author accounts after exact normalized-text deduplication), positiveComments and criticalComments. Different accounts are not proof of different humans or independence. The bounded evidence sample prioritizes qualifying distinct endorsers and retains a critical proof; private selection priorities are stripped before output.

MEASURED: `communityQualitySettings` defaults to 100,000 views, 1,000 likes, any declared audio language and two endorsers. Editable endorser choices are 2/3/5. `verifyCommunityVideos` accepts at most 72 candidates, issues at most two videos.list metadata batches, rejects missing/private/weak statistics or nonmatching selected language, preserves community ranking, and never substitutes a failed/partial check. These fields apply to the current live search and are not portable preferences. Original/archive/imported collections are explicitly separate.

MEASURED: classification data now includes a sanitized requested topic bounded to 120 characters. Community guidance asks for reference rather than positive labels for uncertain relevance, self-promotion or advertisements. Exact quote/schema validation and the three-call budget remain. INFERRED: stricter instructions may improve classification; no live model evaluation was performed, and no authenticity or perfect relevance guarantee is made.
